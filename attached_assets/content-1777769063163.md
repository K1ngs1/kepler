## A decade of turning ideas into apps

From a browser IDE to an AI Agent that ships software in minutes. Thanks to 50M+ creators, the last ten years built the future of coding.

1. 2016



### The beginning







Founded as a humble browser REPL by Amjad Masad, Faris Masad, and Haya Odeh — code in any language, instantly.

2. 2018



### Multiplayer







Multiplayer coding launches. Pair-programming and classrooms move into the browser, natively.

3. 2020



### Always On







Hosting and Always-On Repls go live. Apps stay running, awake to the world, around the clock.

4. 2021



### 10M creators







Bounties launch and the community crosses 10 million creators building together every day.

5. 2022



### Ghostwriter







Replit's first AI coding assistant ships, putting an autocompleting, explaining co-pilot in every workspace.

6. 2023



### Mobile & polish







The Replit mobile app launches alongside a redesigned cloud workspace. Code from anywhere.

7. 2024



### Replit Agent







Replit Agent debuts. Describe an app in plain English and watch it get built end to end.

8. 2025



### 50M creators







More than 50 million people are now building software on Replit across every country on Earth.

9. 2026



### Ten years on







A decade of turning ideas into apps — and the next decade is just getting started.
